## Interpretation Notes

- The assignment document contains duplicated sections with different levels of
  specificity.
- When duplicated sections differ, the project follows the stricter or more
  specific requirement and records that interpretation.
- The revised exact five business actions take precedence over the earlier
  open-ended tool examples.
- Workflow completion is controlled by deterministic Workflow-owned lifecycle
  rules, not an unrestricted AI completion tool.
- Good-to-have features remain optional until all mandatory requirements work.
- Stage 2 `pause` and `interrupt` both block automated inference while retaining
  Signals; `interrupt` specifically represents urgent human review. Either state
  requires `resume` or graceful termination before automation continues.
- Temporal remains the orchestration authority. PostgreSQL is an eventually
  consistent read model populated only through bounded, idempotent Activities;
  exhausted Activity cycles retain their transition and retry after a durable
  timer.
- Stage 3 business actions are local simulations. They execute through an
  allowlisted Activity layer and persist retry-safe evidence, but they do not
  contact real external services.
- Stage 3 changes the serialized supervisor Activity result. Every Workflow
  started by a pre-Stage-3 Worker must be completed or gracefully terminated
  before a Stage 3 Worker polls the shared task queue. The validated local
  upgrade had no open pre-Stage-3 histories; hot replay of one is unsupported.
